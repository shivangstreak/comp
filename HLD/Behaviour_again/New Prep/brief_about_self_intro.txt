2-minute version

“I’m a backend engineer, about nine years in, and most of my career has been in storage and databases.

I joined Nutanix in 2017 and was lucky to be one of the first six engineers on NDB,
their database-as-a-service product. I saw it go from an alpha to a product enterprise customers relied on,
and I grew from MTS1 to MTS3 along the way. Being there from the start meant working across a lot of it: REST APIs and the early UI at first,

then mostly the SQL Server team. That was very Windows- and storage-heavy work.

I built backup and restore flows with VSS and VDI, partly in C++.

I did provisioning on Storage Spaces and dynamic disks, storage extension, and Always On availability groups.
I was also on a lot of customer calls, debugging problems in their environments, which taught me how things actually fail in production.

Since 2023 I’ve been at Oracle, on OCI Object Storage’s metadata services.

It was a deliberate shift from database deployments inside a customer’s environment to a sharded, multi-tenant system at hyperscaler scale.

The project I’m proudest of in my current role is one I owned from design to production: per-bucket metrics that show customers

how much storage their old object versions use. The key decision was to compute them from existing data rather than change

the critical write path, and to make publishing fail-safe so it never shows partial numbers.

I’ve also built Index Shrink, which replaced disruptive full index rebuilds with a lighter in-place approach for most cases,

and adaptive rate limiting so large lifecycle cleanups don’t overload our database shards.

What I enjoy most is the hard part of storage: keeping data correct when things fail, and protecting shared capacity.
Next I’m looking for [your real reason], and that’s what drew me to this role.”
